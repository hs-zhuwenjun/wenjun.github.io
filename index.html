About Me

Mr. Wenjun Zhu is a research engineer within the School of Computer Science at Fudan University.He obtained his Master's degree in software engineering from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications, China. Before joining Fudan University, he worked as a software development engineer at Intel Asia Pacific Research and Development Ltd. He focuses on the optimisation of parallel algorithms for data centre networks and the development of AI-based network frameworks, and was awarded the 2020 Excellent CG Award. His current research interests include converged communications, space-air-ground integrated networks, and network parallel algorithm optimisation systems. More information can be found here..
